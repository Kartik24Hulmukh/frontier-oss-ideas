## One real, consented pilot — protocol proposed, not executed

**Hypothesis [UA]:** a dated alternatives brief adds independently supported evidence to a real developer-tool triage decision beyond the analyst's usual search.

**Owner [OP]:** Yash/founder, subject to explicit acceptance. Research owner recruits two independent reviewers; operator owns safe artifact generation. These are assignments proposed here, not accepted commitments.

**Sample and task:** five actual accelerator analysts with current developer-tool triage duties; four public or participant-approved de-identified lanes each. No friends role-playing analysts, fabricated cases, founder tests counted as users, or new confidential applicants' data. Select four lanes before revealing scores; record inclusion criteria. First consented session is a safety/measurement sentinel within the same five-person pilot, not a separate success cohort.

**Consent prerequisite:** written opt-in for participation and minimal measurement, separately optional permission to publish a case. Explain provider disclosure, public URLs, artifact sharing, withdrawal and deletion. Declining publication must not prevent participation. Contacts stay separate from pseudonymous task records; record an access owner and fixed deletion date, default30days after conclusion unless the participant chooses earlier. Never place raw contact details or private ideas in this report, public proof URLs, git, or a model prompt. If safe de-identification is impossible, decline that task rather than engineer a privacy claim.

**Method:**
1. Capture a real pending decision, pre-choice/rationale and the analyst's normal search process, evidence and elapsed effort. Record the baseline before showing the instrument; use equal time budgets and note order/learning bias.
2. Operator supplies the repaired increment's dated deterministic export, exact model/build, source state and limitations. Human reviewers first check cited evidence support; no raw score alone makes a recommendation. If repair evidence is unavailable, observe baseline only and pause instrument exposure.
3. Analyst inspects the brief without persuasion, records post-choice, decisive links, rejected links and whether evidence was absent from baseline. A justified unchanged decision is valid learning, not a forced pivot.
4. Two reviewers assess the decisive claim/link support independently, blinded to engine score; retain disagreements and adjudication. This is a small utility review, not the separate held-out calibration study.
5. Observe a second **genuine**, user-initiated triage task through day14. A consented outcome interview is not a reminder to use the tool. Record opportunity-to-repeat, not artificially assigned repeat tasks.

**Primary metric:** analysts with at least one concrete changed decision supported by both/adjudicated reviewers and an incremental decisive link / **all five enrolled analysts**. Also publish task completion and supported-unchanged decisions as separate counts. Dropouts remain in the enrolled denominator. This can show instrument contribution, not objectively superior investing or causal time savings.

**Existing pass thresholds preserved:** ≥3/5 supported changed-decision analysts after no more than two iterations; ≥3/5 voluntary repeat on distinct real tasks in week2. If no second task exists, report opportunity-limited/inconclusive repeat evidence, but **do not grant a subscription GO**. Neither small-sample threshold establishes PMF. [^/data/frontier-oss-ideas/docs/LAUNCH_DECISION_2026.md]

**Falsifier:** fewer than3/5 independently supported incremental decision outcomes after two bounded iterations, or analysts consistently find baseline search sufficient and cannot identify additional useful evidence. **Kill rule:** do not launch or add features to rescue a failed protocol; narrow the hypothesis once by explicit decision or pause standalone expansion. Supported non-change can motivate a separately preregistered confirmation-value test—not a post-hoc redefinition of this pass.

**Guardrails:** zero confidential-input exposure, zero fabricated outcomes, no testimonials as compensation conditions, no raw-token/cost claims without evidence; no unsupported buyer-demand, exact competitor-count, independent-invention or investment/novelty conclusions. Record manual effort, support minutes, provider failures and actual incremental costs. Any privacy breach, unsupported decisive claim or unbounded spend pauses the pilot immediately.

**Proposed cap/deadline:**12 founder hours +6 reviewer hours +$100 incremental cash maximum, contingent on approval; no paid acquisition. These are planning caps, not known costs/runway. Freeze protocol within24hours of owner acceptance; close recruitment within7days; outcome review14days after first consented session, with a stated outer deadline21days after acceptance. No qualified consenting participants by recruitment deadline is an access failure, not evidence the product works or fails. No deadline pressure permits fabricated completion.
