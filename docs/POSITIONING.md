# Positioning — Simultaneity Index

## One-liner
Live crowding radar: how many teams are already inventing your idea — independently.

## Category
**Crowding intelligence** (supply-side simultaneity detection)

Not:
- Market research suite  
- AI business plan generator  
- Customer interview platform  
- Generic idea validator  

## ICP (in order)
1. Solo technical founders / vibe-coders about to spend a weekend  
2. Indie hackers comparing 3–5 ideas  
3. AI coding agents that need a pre-scaffold tool  
4. Research / strategy academics studying parallel invention  
5. Accelerators doing batch idea triage  

## Problem
Shipping is cheap. **Knowing whether the lane is already full of simultaneous builders is hard.**  
Existing tools either hallucinate competitors or bury you in 150-page reports.

## Promise
In under a minute, see:
- Crowding score 0–100  
- Verdict band  
- Confidence  
- Clickable evidence across ecosystems  
- Open wedge suggestions  

## Differentiation table

| | LLM validators | Pain miners | Viability suites | **Simultaneity Index** |
|--|----------------|-------------|------------------|------------------------|
| Live sources | Rare | Yes | Yes (paid) | **Yes (free core)** |
| Clickable evidence | No | Partial | Yes | **Always** |
| Supply-side builders | Weak | No | Partial | **Core** |
| Pain/demand | Weak | Core | Yes | Later optional |
| Weight | Light | Medium | Heavy | **Ultra-light** |
| Agent-native | Rare | No | No | **Yes (roadmap)** |

## Messaging pillars
1. **Evidence, not vibes**  
2. **Simultaneity is the default**  
3. **Find the wedge**  
4. **Built for humans and agents**  

## Brand voice
Instrument panel. Monospace. Calm. Honest. Slightly scientific. Never hype-bro.

## Anti-positioning (say out loud)
- Not investment advice  
- Heuristic crowding, not legal novelty opinion  
- Partial sources reduce confidence, not silence
