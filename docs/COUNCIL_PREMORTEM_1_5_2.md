## Debate
- Research objected that integrity metadata does not validate measurement; proposed an independently reproducible audit instrument.
- Reliability challenged the absence of chaos and external provenance evidence. Its suggestion that the failed timeout was “noise” is rejected: the observed failure stays a release risk until explained.
- Founder advocated radical scope reduction to one vertical and one adapter, challenging breadth without pilots. Cutting adapters is not adopted without evidence; it would change the product promise and may hide rather than solve blind spots.
- Research's September 1 deadline was already past on the September 26 run date. It is not accepted as a usable launch schedule. Proposed numerical criteria in model replies are suggestions, not observed results or replacements for existing preregistration.

## Chair decision
Merge tested provenance fixes as a research-beta increment. No billing, guaranteed capacity, mass outreach or production-readiness claim. Keep the strict gate red rather than treating fallback as source recovery. Preserve failed gateway evidence alongside any repeat. Next high-leverage product experiment: consented analyst decision replay in a narrow vertical, with pre/post decisions and independently judged evidence. Measure changed decisions and voluntary week-two return, not scan count.
