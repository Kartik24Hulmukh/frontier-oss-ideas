## Strategic diagnosis and launch wedge

### Three genuinely different options

- **A — Analyst evidence triage [OP; recommended]:** small consented pilot, existing downloadable brief, public/de-identified lane text. Best near-term path to observing a concrete decision and a potential buyer; procurement, low frequency and confidentiality are risks.
- **B — Builder/MCP pre-scaffold check [OP; secondary test]:** low-friction utility inside an existing coding workflow. Potentially recurring across projects, but easily substituted and already contested; an install does not prove use.
- **C — Public metric/leaderboard [OP; defer as primary bet]:** potentially shareable editorial content. Weakest near-term connection to buying or better decisions; requires longitudinal comparability that current re-scanning share pages do not establish.

[OP] A wins because it tests utility and commercial plausibility without building a SaaS platform. If repeat analyst use fails but developer MCP pull succeeds, switch to B deliberately. Do not run all three as equal priorities.

**Proposed promise:** “Before approving the next build, inspect relevant public alternatives and a falsifiable wedge in one evidence-linked brief.”

**Do not promise:** exact team counts, verified buyer demand, legal novelty, investment selection, “Blue Ocean” certainty, or a probability that an idea will succeed. Discussion heat is not willingness to pay; a quiet source is not an empty market. [^docs/METHODOLOGY.md] [^docs/LAUNCH_DECISION_2026.md]

**Workflow:** record initial build/stop/reframe decision → inspect relevant evidence → name one decisive link and counterexample → record resulting decision, including justified non-change → optionally export a dated brief → return on the next real triage task.

**Pilot primary metric [OP]:** proportion of participating analysts able to document a concrete evidence-supported changed decision. Also retain well-supported decisions that did not change; otherwise the product is rewarded for persuading people to pivot unnecessarily. Do not call a self-report objectively better judgment without independent evidence review.

**100× leverage [WI]:** a permissioned corpus of independently adjudicated relevance errors and decision outcomes, linked to model/version/coverage, could improve the brief and make distribution artifacts credible. More anonymous scans alone do not create that corpus or a network effect.

## Distribution experiments — proposals, not executed results

All budgets below are proposed founder-time/cash caps, not known runway. No outreach or publication is authorized or performed by this review. Founder must separately approve recruitment, posts and integration submissions. Treat small samples as directional learning, not statistical proof.

### E1 — Decision-replay pilot: primary experiment

- **Question/hypothesis [UA]:** accelerator analysts evaluating developer-tool lanes obtain evidence they would not otherwise use and return for another real triage task.
- **Target/sample:** five consented analysts; four public or safely de-identified lanes each. User and potential buyer initially overlap; actual budget authority must be checked.
- **Method:** first capture their usual search process, elapsed effort and pre-decision. Deliver existing evidence brief without leading explanation. Capture decisive links, rejected links, false positives, post-decision, elapsed effort and willingness to repeat. Two independent reviewers assess relevance/support blind to score. Do not solicit testimonials as the condition of participation.
- **Owner/budget/deadline:** founder owns pilot; research owner owns adjudication; cap 12 founder hours and $100 incremental costs; prepare by 8 October, initial results by 13 October, unprompted repeat window through 20 October. Dates are a proposed reset, not a backfilled September accomplishment.
- **Primary metric:** analysts with a concrete evidence-supported changed decision / five enrolled analysts. Report completion separately; a missing participant is not silently removed from the denominator.
- **Success:** retain existing gate of at least 3/5 naming changed decisions after at most two iterations, plus at least 3/5 voluntarily repeating by week two. [^docs/LAUNCH_DECISION_2026.md]
- **Guardrails:** no confidential ideas sent to providers; no unreviewed competitor accusations; record coverage; no payment/capacity promises; reviewers must reject unsupported decisive links.
- **Failure/falsifier:** fewer than 3/5 utility outcomes after two iterations, or participants say ordinary search achieves the same result without material extra effort.
- **Decision:** utility + repeat → limited public beta; utility without repeat → episodic concierge tool, no subscription; neither → revise ICP once or pause. Stop after two pilot iterations rather than adding retention features to hide weak pull.

### E2 — One MCP workflow, not a custom agent distribution

- **Hypothesis [UA]:** an explicit, opt-in pre-scaffold check is actually invoked and its evidence reviewed, not just configured.
- **Method/sample:** after operational admission is safe, test one supported client with five consenting developer-tool builders. Record install attempts, successful initialize/discovery/tool call, relevant evidence review and a second user-initiated task within 14 days. First two attempts are observed cold installs; no credentials supplied by this reviewer.
- **Owner/cap/deadline:** growth owner + technical maintainer; six hours/$50; start after gate review, conclude within 14 days of start.
- **Success [OP]:** 4/5 complete the first evidence review; 3/5 voluntarily invoke again on a distinct relevant task. Do not use automated tool-call count as retention.
- **Failure/falsifier:** fewer than 3/5 finish review after one installation correction, or generic web/GitHub search is consistently preferred.
- **Guardrails:** no automatic blocking of scaffolding based solely on score; explicit opt-in, easy removal, no silent config writes; no demand or receipt-trust overclaim.
- **Next:** pass → prepare one maintainer-reviewed extension recipe and one registry submission for founder approval; installation failure → fix documentation only; utility failure → stop integration proliferation.

### E3 — Reproducible evidence artifact → qualified review

- **Hypothesis [UA]:** a named, transparent case attracts people with a real imminent build/triage decision, not only curiosity clicks.
- **Method:** only after E1 utility and operational gates, prepare one consented case study with dated export, coverage, model version, decisive links, rejected evidence and before/after decision. One small Show HN or relevant developer community post, following current community rules, is a future founder-approved action. No coordinated voting, seeded testimonials or newsletter blast.
- **Owner/cap/window:** founder; four hours/$0 paid promotion; seven days after publication.
- **Metric:** qualified visitors completing an evidence review on their own real lane / qualified landing visitors who consent to measurement; log numerator and denominator. Report pseudonymous unique users separately from requests.
- **Directional pass [OP]:** at least five qualified completed reviews and two voluntary returns/referrals. Fewer than five qualified visitors is a reach failure/inconclusive utility test; at least 20 qualified visitors but fewer than two completed reviews falsifies the current artifact/promise sufficiently to stop this channel version.
- **Guardrails/next:** count neither bots, founder tests nor badge image fetches as users; cap capacity; preserve source failures. Pass → repeat once with a new evidence case; fail → one copy/targeting correction, then stop. Referral attribution is not guaranteed merely because a badge exists.

Do not let that become another month of signing/router work without user observation. Run consented observation of the ordinary search baseline in parallel, then show repaired evidence.
