## One ICP, one wedge, one metric

### ICP — recommended hypothesis, not validated segmentation

**[OP/UA] User:** an accelerator or venture-studio analyst who is currently reviewing a small shortlist of **public or explicitly consented OSS/devtool software ideas** and must write an evidence-linked build/stop/reframe memo. **Buyer:** the program/venture lead who owns the analyst's time and approves a pilot. Qualify only someone with a real current shortlist, existing comparison workflow, authority or access to the buyer, and a plausible second review cycle. Do not start with general funds, academics, every solo founder and every coding agent simultaneously.

**Pain hypothesis:** time spent separating relevant public substitutes from lexical noise and defending the resulting shortlist—not needing a machine to decide whether an applicant is investable. **[NE]** Frequency, severity, cost of manual work, procurement cycle and budget remain unmeasured. The repository's own narrower launch plan supports testing analysts; it does not validate this proposed devtools subsegment. [^../../docs/LAUNCH_DECISION_2026.md#L24-L27] [^../../docs/POSITIONING.md#L15-L20]

### Wedge

**[OP] One four-lane decision review packet:** baseline shortlist choice → existing scan → human inspection of relevant substitutes and differences → dated brief with decisive links, source limitations and one falsification test → recorded final choice. Output is **a reviewable crowding memo**, not novelty certification, applicant rejection, verified demand, or a market census. Use the existing cohort/brief/CSV capabilities; no 300-item dashboard, PDF crawl service or enterprise account build is required to test this job. The current demo explicitly caps batches at 25 and says background 200–500 screening and white-label PDFs are unavailable. [^../../lib/brief.ts#L41-L55] [^../../app/funds/page.tsx#L14-L27]

### Core pre-PMF metric

**[OP] Evidence-backed completed decision reviews per participating organization per review cycle**, reported as **completed reviews / all assigned eligible reviews**. A counted review needs a recorded pre-decision, post-decision, human-inspected decisive evidence link(s), rationale, reviewer and date; identify whether the choice changed or was corroborated. Count unique lane decisions, not rescans or model calls. Report withdrawals, unusable results and insufficient-evidence abstentions separately rather than deleting them from the denominator.

Use the preregistered **3/5 analysts with a concrete changed decision** and **3/5 voluntary week-two repeat** as pilot continuation gates, not a statistical guarantee. Keep unchanged-but-helpful corroboration separate; do not incentivize manufacturing a "kill" to inflate decision-change. Scan counts, shares, API invocations and citations are diagnostic channel metrics only. Do not christen a mature North Star before actual usage exists. ## Bounded experiment — no invented validation

**Hypothesis [UA]:** a qualified analyst can use this evidence packet to make or materially reframe an actual shortlist decision more efficiently than their existing manual search, and voluntarily use it on a second shortlist.

- **Sample/method:** five consented analysts, four eligible lanes each. Record baseline decisions and the manual workflow before showing score/brief; human-review decisive links, abstentions, false positives and final decisions. Twenty lane reviews are repeated observations nested within five analysts—not twenty independent customers. Where feasible counterbalance manual/tool review order to reduce novelty and order effects.
- **Owner:** founder for recruitment/utility and a separate research lead for evaluation. **Proposed budget:** at most 20 founder hours for two cycles; no ad spend, mass outreach or new paid infrastructure purchased by this reviewer. Confirm founder time/runway before committing.
- **Deadline:** start only after consent/provider-handling and safe beta capacity are established; first review within seven days of pilot start, voluntary second-cycle observation within fourteen days. Two correction cycles maximum before a continuation decision.
- **Primary:** completed evidence-backed reviews / eligible assigned reviews; additionally the existing 3/5 concrete changed-decision gate. **Guardrails:** no confidential applicant leakage, no fabricated citations, inspectable relevant support, measured time versus manual baseline, source-failure rate, per-review delivery cost and support time. A faster but unsupported or wrong recommendation is not success.
- **Continue:** at least 3/5 can identify a concrete changed decision after at most two iterations, at least 3/5 repeat voluntarily in week two, and relevance/operational gates remain acceptable. Log all negative results. The existing commercial gate additionally asks for WTP interviews and three written commitments; none is a paid-production certification.
- **Falsifier/kill:** analysts do not have a frequent costly decision job, outputs are merely interesting, decisive links do not support conclusions, or fewer than 3/5 show utility or voluntary repeat after two iterations. Stop the subscription/large-cohort roadmap and reconsider ICP; do not respond by adding accounts and reminder emails.
